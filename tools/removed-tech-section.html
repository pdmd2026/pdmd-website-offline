The projection itself, then every number behind it.

Technical details

DMD pushes the student endpoint along the negative of the teacher–critic difference. The critic is online: it chases a student that moves at every step, never tracks it exactly, and its estimation error rides into the student and compounds.

d  =  scritic − steacher      d⊥  =  d − (⟨d, r⟩ / ‖r‖²)·r

Why that direction

At a fixed noisy query, r = x0c − x0s is an unbiased estimate of the critic's endpoint error. The part of the update along r is exactly the contaminated part.

Why the signal survives

In high dimension the matching signal and one residual direction are close to orthogonal, so deleting one direction costs almost nothing. The step still descends: ⟨d⊥, d⟩ = ‖d⊥‖² ≥ 0.

What it costs

An inner product and a subtraction on tensors already in memory. No auxiliary loss, no discriminator, no extra evaluation, no second stage.
